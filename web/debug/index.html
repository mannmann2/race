How these numbers are measured

Visitors are daily estimates based on the connection’s IP address and broad browser type. Shared networks can merge visitors; changing IPs or devices can count one person more than once. Identifiers rotate daily and raw IPs are not stored. Sessions reset after 30 minutes without recorded activity or at midnight.

Active time counts a focused, visible page and pauses after two minutes without interaction. Background tabs are excluded and overlapping tabs are not counted twice. Session span includes short gaps between activity. Very short visits may have zero measured duration. Known bots, browser privacy opt-outs and visits with blocked measurement scripts are excluded.
