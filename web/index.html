 BEHIND THE STARTING LINES

One world.
A lot of ways to run it.

Race Atlas is an independent race discovery tool for exploring the UTMB Index, the ITRA calendar and World’s Marathons. It is not affiliated with these organisations. ITRA coverage is shown above; unavailable imports do not affect the UTMB catalogue.

Locations are approximate

The UTMB feed provides place names, not starting-line coordinates. Markers show matching towns from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Names with multiple matches use the largest locality in that country and may be ambiguous. Races without a matched town remain in the calendar. Always check the organiser’s website for the exact start.

Category ≠ distance

20K, 50K, 100K and 100M are UTMB effort categories, not exact distances. The “42” option selects full-marathon listings and races recorded at 42–42.3 km by any source, regardless of UTMB category. Selecting multiple buttons includes races matching any selected option; other filters still apply. Use the distance and elevation filters to find the actual course profile. “No UTMB category” means the source feed supplied a null category; the race has no UTMB category supplied, including races listed only by ITRA or World’s Marathons. World Series includes source levels serie, major and finale.

Grade colors

Calendar cards and race details show both official grade badges when supplied, UTMB then ITRA. Their accent uses the UTMB category color when supplied, otherwise the ITRA Endurance Points color. Missing grades stay gray. Map bubbles use a muted palette, with green groups in light mode and gold groups in dark mode.

ITRA ratings and shared listings

Endurance Points (0–6), Mountain Level (0–12) and Finisher Level come from ITRA course listings. Missing ratings are unknown, not zero. Clear matches for the same course and edition share one card and link to both sources. Uncertain matches remain separate. Conflicting source measurements are shown in race details. ITRA listings marked as indexed come from older public search-index copies, with limited coverage. Their retrieval date is not a live verification date; check the linked race page for changes.

World’s Marathons

Full-marathon events come from the public search calendar, refreshed weekly for the current year and following two years. Entries without an explicit marathon distance are excluded. Dates and distances describe event listings; separate same-distance courses may not be identified. Surface labels come from this provider, and unknown surfaces are not inferred. Locations are approximate event coordinates or town matches. Shared listings retain every source link.

A snapshot, ready to explore

Filters run on your device. Data is a downloaded snapshot, not a live registration feed; dates and courses can change. Images come from UTMB’s World Series catalogue , ITRA event pages, World’s Marathons listings and Open Graph metadata on official event websites. Coverage varies; missing or unavailable photos are hidden. Images may represent the event rather than a specific year. With “Only races in map view” enabled, unmapped races and the far side of the globe are excluded; turn it off to browse all matches.

Privacy & feedback

Searches and filters run in your browser. Only your theme preference is saved locally; no account or analytics service is used. Our server keeps operational request logs. Map tiles and optional race photos load from external providers, which receive your IP address and browser request information. Fonts are hosted here. Race locations are approximate, and registration or cancellation status is not verified.
